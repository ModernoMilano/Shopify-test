## What is different from the earlier batches

- **Five ads use no generated image.** 04 and 05 are typography only; 08, 09 and 10 use the real Shopify photos, cut out of their studio background. The product is exact by construction.
- **New formats:** a ranked list, a question (question hooks did best in the account's copy), an annotated product, and a covered discount number.
- **Composition is set in the prompt.** The light product shots (06, 07) were generated with an empty wall above the product, so the text sits on a calm area without mirrored or stretched edges.
- **One generation round, no retakes.** All five generated images passed the check on the first try.

## Check before running

- **26.11.** in ad 04 comes from the winning ad ("Sealed until 26.11."). Confirm that date still stands.
- **"Our biggest sale ever"** must be true for this Black Friday.
- **Ad 05** shows a % sign with the number covered. It promises a percentage discount without naming it.
- **Ad 10** names "white-trimmed hood" and "drawstring waist". Both are visible in the product photo; the other four callouts are the confirmed product facts.
- Teasers (01–05) link to the List signup page; product ads (06–10) link to The Bestsellers.
