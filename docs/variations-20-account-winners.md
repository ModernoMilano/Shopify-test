## Rules applied

- No discounts, no strikethrough prices and no "limited stock". Prices are today's Shopify prices.
- No fabric claims beyond the verified facts. The Mezza Zip, Imperial and Exclusive are not shown here.
- **Remade before delivery:**
  - the train shot, where a face was reflected in the window;
  - the Notte price flat lay, after a false NSFW flag.
- **Fixed in the crop:** in 06–08 the lips were visible; they were cropped away at the chin.
- **Check before running:** 18 says "Folded, boxed". That line comes from your own winning Unboxing ad. Use it only if orders really ship in a box.
