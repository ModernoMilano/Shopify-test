# ModernoMilano Audience Audit

*October 2026. Data: Meta Ads for the last 6 months (€86k spend, 1,196 attributed purchases) and Shopify since launch (May 2026: 1,756 orders, 1,648 customers, €265k net sales). It also uses the creative analysis of 767 ads.*

---

## Key findings

1. **The buyer is a 25–44 year-old man in a big city, shopping on an iPhone through Instagram.**
   - Men 25–34 bring 41% of purchases at the best ROAS (2.46x).
   - 94% of Meta purchases happen on an iPhone.
   - Instagram is the biggest order source and gets 89% of ad spend.
2. **He buys a complete outfit, not a single piece.**
   - Customers buy 1.68 items per order.
   - AOV rose from €85 in May to €195 in October.
   - Bundle and complete-look ads beat single-product ads by about 2x.
3. **He buys at full price.**
   - 93% of orders use no discount code.
   - Discount-led creative is the weakest angle (summer sale 1.62x).
   - A price anchor works (*€175, was €300*); a promo code does not.
4. **He buys in the evening, on the sofa.**
   - Orders peak between 19:00 and 01:00, highest at 21:00.
   - Saturday is the strongest day.
5. **Most money is lost after the click, in Germany and France.**
   - Germany brings the most traffic, but only 40% of German checkouts complete.
   - In France only 27% complete, at a ROAS of 1.50x.

## 5. Leaks and risks

1. **German checkout drop-off.**
   - The funnel: 1,526 carts → 876 checkouts → 350 orders in 90 days.
   - Fix it with German-language creative and checkout trust signals.
   - Apple Pay, Google Pay and Shop Pay are already enabled. Show them, because 94% of buyers are on an iPhone.
2. **France underperforms.**
   - ROAS is 1.50x and only 27% of checkouts complete.
   - Pause broad French spend until French-language creative is live.
3. **The refund policy contradicts the ads.**
   - The policy promises 30-day returns, but it also says *"we cannot accept returns on sale items"*. Every product shows a compare-at price, so customers will read every product as being on sale.
   - The policy still contains a placeholder (`[INSERT RETURN ADDRESS]`).
   - The Terms of Service still contain placeholders.
   - The policy pages use two different contact email addresses.
   - **Fix all of this before running risk-reversal ads.** A trust ad that leads to a contradictory policy page loses trust.
4. **About 2,000 people left their details but never bought.**
   - There are 3,835 customer records against 1,648 buyers.
   - This is the cheapest audience you own. Use it for retargeting and email.
5. **Men 45–54 and Facebook feed/Reels drag down the average.** Reduce their spend.

| # | Angle | Psychological lever | Target | Hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | **"We did the matching for you"** | Removes decision fatigue | Men 25–44, UK and DE | *Stop guessing what goes with what.* |

| C | **Comfort that still looks sharp** | Permission and reward | Men 25–44, evenings and weekends | *Comfortable enough for the sofa. Sharp enough for dinner.* |
| D | **The gift he'll actually wear** | Removes gift stress | Women 30–65, Nov–Dec | *He's impossible to buy for. Until now.* |
| E | **No risk** | Loss aversion, social proof, own language | DE and FR retargeting, the 2,000 non-buyers | *30 Tage Rückgaberecht.* / *1,700+ orders since May.* |
