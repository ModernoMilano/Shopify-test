## How it's built

- **Formats:** one 9:16 image of 2160×3840. Product, headline and logo all sit inside the centre square (y 840–3000), so the 1:1 is an exact centre crop with nothing important lost. The 9:16 keeps the top 14% and bottom 20% free of text.
- **Images:** Nano Banana Pro, generated from the real product photos. The colour grids (22, 23) are the real Shopify photos themselves.
- **Typography:** a serif headline of 6 words or fewer, plus one small line in wide-spaced caps, and the real logo. The text is cream or black, sits only on calm areas, and has no shadows or gradients.
- **No faces:** backs, silhouettes, hands, a phone covering the face in the UGC shot, or product only.
- **Claims (PRODUCT FACTS):**
  - **Lounge Set:** only the name, €150, and "sized separately S–2XL". There is no fabric claim.
  - **Two-Tone:** only the confirmed facts (cashmere blend knit, contrast stripe, ribbed cuffs, relaxed tapered fit).
  - **Service:** free shipping, 30-day returns, dispatch in 1–3 business days.

## Quality control (rejected and remade before delivery)

- **Collage / band artefacts:** 04, 05, 06, 10, 11, 12, 16 (a frame inside a frame) were remade or fixed.
- **Headless bodies:** 29 and 31 were remade as product-only.
- **Faces / AI artefacts:** in 06 the face was visible, and in 16 the chin melted into a blur. Both were remade.
- **Product fidelity:**
  - Two-Tone with a stand collar instead of the real hood with white trim: 05, 15, 25, 32, 38 were all remade with the real hood.
  - The round-1 static `MM_TheQuietFlex_45_v1` had the same error (I wrongly "corrected" it to a stand collar). The fixed version is in the zip as `MM_TheQuietFlex_45_v2_CORRECTED`.
- **Brand in the background:** none visible. Plane, shop signs and labels were all checked.
