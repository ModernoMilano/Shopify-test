# ModernoMilano · Round 1 · 12 concepts

**Statics (4:5):** [MM_Round1_Statics.zip](https://d2ol7oe51mr4n9.cloudfront.net/user_3EYCP9eOPYPhvDZtzEySAqbjgsC/da8abc4a-37fd-441a-81ad-0eabc3702abc.zip). It holds 11 finished statics plus an overview sheet. MM10 has no static, because it needs real reviews.

**Rules I applied:**
- Every product claim comes from PRODUCT FACTS.
  - **Lounge Set:** only the product name "The Milano Cashmere Lounge Set" and the price. There is no fabric or detail claim.
  - **Two-Tone set:** only the confirmed facts: cashmere blend knit, contrast side stripe, ribbed cuffs and hem, two side pockets, relaxed tapered fit.
  - **Hooded Jacket:** no fabric claims, only the name and the service line.
- **No faces:** the shots use backs, crops from the collarbone down, or hands only.
- **Typography:** a serif headline of 6 words or fewer, plus one small line in wide-spaced caps. That is two fonts. The text is cream or black, sits in the calm third, and has no shadow or gradient.
- **Service line:** where proof is needed, the small line reads "Free shipping · 30-day returns".
- **Video:** every video is 10–15 s, with a cut every 1–1.5 s. There is a 6 s cut-down made from shots 1, 2 and the end card. The end card is the logo plus one line.
