# BF50: 25 Black Friday teasers + 25 product ads (9:16 + 1:1)

**Download:** [MM_BF50_Ads.zip](https://d2ol7oe51mr4n9.cloudfront.net/user_3EYCP9eOPYPhvDZtzEySAqbjgsC/1db986fc-5fc3-4749-a636-c1dfe10a2d26.zip). It holds 101 files: 50 ads as `_916` (1080×1920) and `_11` (1080×1080), plus `MM_BF_00_Overview.jpg`.

**Sheet:** `deliverables/BF50/BF50_SHEET.xlsx` (or `.csv`). Per ad it lists the type, what is shown, the text in the image, the button, primary texts (short and medium), the Meta headline, the description and the link.

## How it's split

### T01–T25: Black Friday teasers (50%)

These are mysterious and **never show a discount**. Copy lines include:
- "You don't want to miss this."
- "Our biggest sale ever."
- "The number stays secret."
- "Sealed until Black Friday."
- "Something big is coming."
- "Almost time."

**Visuals:** black boxes with light leaking out, curtains, wax seals, a keyhole, a locked vitrine, a price tag face down, an hourglass, the Galleria at night, a rainy shop window.

**Styles:**
- 18 in the dark winner style (cursive serif with a button);
- 3 in big bold caps (T12, T16, T25);
- 3 light variants (T22–T24);
- 1 with the text at the top (T10).

**Button:** "JOIN THE LIST". These ads should link to the List signup or early-access page, not straight to the shop.

### P01–P25: product ads without Black Friday (50%)

Light, daytime and lifestyle, following the account winners:
- clean product on grey;
- back views while travelling or in Milan;
- flat lays;
- UGC mirror and flat-lay captions;
- a catalog built from real Shopify photos.

10 of them have no headline (clean or logo only), as a test.

**Link:** The Bestsellers. Prices are today's Shopify prices.

## Rules applied

- **No discount** anywhere: no percentages, no strikethrough prices.
- **"Biggest sale ever"** is the user's own line. Use it only if this Black Friday really is the biggest so far (EU rules on superlatives).
- **Faces:** none. People are shown from behind, as silhouettes, as hands, or with a phone in front of the face. One shot was cropped above the mouth.
- **Not called cashmere:** Imperial and Exclusive.
- **Lounge Set:** its name only, no fabric claims.
- **Two-Tone:** only the confirmed facts.
- **Images:** Nano Banana Pro at 2K (about 104 credits).
- **Remade:**
  - a collage band on the envelope (T23);
  - two arms in the doorway (T24);
  - a ghost artefact on the four-shades stack (P19).
