**Sheet:** `deliverables/RA20/RA20_SHEET.xlsx` (or `.csv`). Per creative it lists the product shown, the text in the image, primary texts (short and medium), the Meta headline and the description.

## How it's built

- **Format:** 2160×3840. Product, headline and logo sit inside the centre square (y 840–3000), so the 1:1 is an exact centre crop.
- **Images:** Nano Banana Pro, generated from the real Reverso Shopify photos. 04, 05 and 20 use the real Shopify flat lays themselves.
- **Typography:** a Cormorant headline (6 words or fewer), one Montserrat caps line, and the real logo. No shadows, gradients or "was" prices.
- **Faces:** none are shown. People appear from behind, as hands only, or cropped by the frame.
- **Claims:** only verified Shopify facts are used:
  - the reversible gilet;
  - 12 Quarter Zip colours;
  - Quarter Zip set €220 and Long Sleeve set €175;
  - the gilet and trousers are wrinkle-resistant and quick-drying;
  - free shipping, 30-day returns, dispatch in 1–3 business days.

  The knit gets no fabric claims.

**Formats:**
- **9:16:** full bleed.
- **4:5 and 1:1:** the full figure, with white added at the sides. The neck cut stays at the top edge, so the body never floats without a head.

## Quality control (remade before delivery)

- **Inset panel:** the macro (07) came back as a frame inside a frame and was remade.
- **Wrong fabric:** the reversed gilet (W5) came back as shiny leather. It was remade in the real matte brown.
- **Faces:**
  - Chin and mouth were visible on W1–W5. They were cropped away at the neck.
  - In 06, 11, 12, 14, 19, the figure was too large, so the 1:1 crop cut off the feet. These were remade with a smaller figure.
- **Text over the product:** in 01 and 17 the text or logo touched the garment. The image was shifted down; for 17, the small line moved to the copy.
