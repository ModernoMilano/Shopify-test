# Retargeting: response to the creative brief + batch 1

*October 2026. All facts below were checked in Shopify on 26-10-2026.*

## First: where we push back

Fix points 1 and 2 before launch. They cost more conversions than any creative can win back.

1. **The landing pages break the fit and trust concepts.** Retargeting sends warm people straight to the product page, and three of the five best sellers have a broken one:
   - **Cashmere Lounge Set Beige** (our #1 product) has an **empty product description**: no material, no size guide, no shipping or returns text.
   - **Cashmere Lounge Set Black, Gray and Navy** show the description and size guide of the **Two-Tone Cashmere Set**, a different product with contrast stripes and a drawstring.

   Add the correct material, size guide and the returns/shipping block to all four before the fit concept runs. Today a "know your size" ad for the Lounge Set leads to a page without sizes.
2. **The live refund policy contradicts "30-day returns".**
   - It still says *"we cannot accept returns on sale items"*. Every product shows a compare-at price, so customers read everything as being on sale.
   - It still contains `[INSERT RETURN ADDRESS]` and the old iCloud email address.
   - The corrected text is ready in `docs/policies/refund-policy.html`. Paste it into Shopify before RT1 and RT6 go live.
3. **The price-breakdown idea backfires.**
   - Reverso Sabbia bought separately (Reverso gilet €70 + Supima tee €40 + Classico pants €60) is **€170**, so it is cheaper than the €175 set.
   - The Lounge Set pieces are not sold separately at all, so there is nothing to compare.
   - I replaced the idea with a **"what you get"** concept (RT4) that sells completeness, not savings.
4. **Some brief numbers don't match Shopify.**
   - **Reverso Set:** the best seller is the **Reverso Sabbia Set at €175, was €255**, not €300.
   - **Dark Mocha:** it has a compare-at price of €300.
   - **Returns:** Shopify shows €11.5k returns on €256.6k gross sales in the last 90 days, which is **4.5%, not 13%**. Please check where the 13% comes from; it may include cancellations or refunds handled outside Shopify's returns flow. Fit is still worth solving, but it is not as big a fire as the brief suggests.
5. **The UAE is missing from the country list.** It is the #1 market for the Reverso Set and has the highest AOV (€260). Add AE to the English ad set.
6. **Keep prices out of the images and the copy.**
   - Norway and Poland see NOK and PLN, not EUR. For example, the Lounge Set is 1,594 NOK and 674 PLN.
   - Strike-through prices carry a legal risk in the EU (Omnibus rule: the "was" price must be the lowest price of the previous 30 days). A permanent compare-at price is risky, especially in Germany. Check this with your advisor before you use "was" prices in DE ads.
   - All batch 1 creatives are therefore price-free.
7. **Payment methods.**
   - Shopify confirms **Shop Pay, Apple Pay and Google Pay**.
   - **Klarna** is not visible through the API. Confirm it in your checkout and I will add "Klarna" to RT1 (5 minutes).
8. **Cashmere wording.** The products are a **cashmere blend**. Never write "pure cashmere" or "100% cashmere".
9. **Budget and frequency.**
   - At €40/day and roughly €10–15 CPM you buy about 19–28k impressions per week. Delivery concentrates on the hottest people (cart and checkout), so their frequency will be well above the pool average.
   - Run **one ad set** with **at most 6–8 ads live**, and rotate every 2 weeks.
   - Consider shortening video viewers from 120 to 30 days. A 120-day video view is the weakest intent in the pool.

## Concepts that need real material from you

I did not fake these, because a fake version would make the hesitation worse.

1. **On-body try-on (fit).** A real person wearing the Lounge Set and the Reverso Sabbia Set, with **height, weight and size worn** on screen.
   - **Needed:** a 10–15 second phone video per set, plus the person's real height and size.
2. **Reviews / UGC (trust).**
   - **Needed:** 8–10 real reviews, each with first name, country, product, size bought and permission to use it in ads. Photos are optional and a big plus.
3. **Unboxing (quality and trust).**
   - **Needed:** one real unboxing video of the actual packaging, 10–15 seconds, hands only is fine.

## Setup advice

- **Ad sets:** one ad set; run EN and DE as language versions within the same ads (multiple languages).
- **Countries:** add **AE** to the country list.
- **Start with 6 ads:** RT1 Lounge Beige, RT1 Reverso Sabbia, RT2 Lounge Beige, RT3 Dark Mocha, RT5 Dark Mocha, plus RT6 catalog.
  - After 7 days, swap the weakest for RT1 Hooded Jacket, RT2 Sabbia or RT4.
  - RT1 and RT6 wait for the refund policy fix.
- **Success:** retargeting ROAS of 3 or higher, and a frequency of 3 per week or less per segment.
