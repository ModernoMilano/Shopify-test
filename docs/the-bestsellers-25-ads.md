## Built on the winner ("Sealed until 26.11.")

These ads reuse the winner's look:
- **Image:** a near-black, low-key still life of one product on black marble, with a single narrow beam of warm light across the knit.
- **Top of the text block:** the cream script logo, with a small spaced label underneath (`THE BESTSELLERS` instead of `THE LIST`).
- **Headline:** a large italic serif (Cormorant Garamond Italic) over two lines.
- **Line:** two calm lines of sans text (Inter).
- **Button:** one cream button with wide-spaced bold caps (`SHOP THE BESTSELLERS`).

**What's different:** logo, product, headline and button all sit inside the centre square (y 420–1500 of 1920), so the 1:1 is an exact centre crop. In 9:16, no text falls in the top 14% or bottom 20%.

## Rules applied

- **Ranking:** N°1–N°10 follow your list. It matches the manual order of the collection in Shopify.
- **Prices:** today's Shopify prices only. There are no discounts, no Black Friday, no "Bundle & Save" wording in the image, and no crossed-out prices.
- **Not called cashmere:** the Mezza Zip, the Imperial Zip Set and the Exclusive Lounge Set.
- **Lounge Set:** only its name is used, with no fabric claims.
- **Two-Tone:** only the confirmed facts: cashmere blend knit, contrast stripe, ribbed cuffs, relaxed tapered fit.
- **Images:** Nano Banana Pro, generated from the real Shopify product photos. Every product was checked against its photo; the Signature Polo came out grey and was remade in cream.
