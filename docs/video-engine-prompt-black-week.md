# Prompt for the creative video engine: ModernoMilano Black Week 2026

Copy everything below the line into the video engine.

---

You are producing paid-social videos (Meta Reels/Stories, TikTok) for **ModernoMilano**, a premium Italian-inspired menswear brand. Deliver **one-shot, final-quality** videos. Every frame must look like a real, high-end product film shot on a cinema camera, never like AI. If a shot cannot meet the rules below, simplify the shot; never deliver a flawed one.

**Quality benchmark:** match the professionalism of the previous videos you made for ModernoMilano. Those were excellent, and they are the standard for this brief. Use the same production quality, polish, lighting, colour grading, pacing, camera work, editing rhythm, transitions and finishing. This brief only changes the content (events, offers and shots), not the quality bar. When in doubt, do it the way you did it in those videos.

**Scope:** only **5 videos**, one per event, each delivered with two hooks. Put all effort into making these 5 perfect rather than making more.

## 1. Global rules (apply to every video)

**Format**
- 9:16, 1080×1920 minimum (4K preferred), 25 or 30 fps, 9–12 seconds.
- Safe zone: all product and all text inside the central square (vertical 22%–78% of the frame). Keep nothing important in the top 14% or bottom 20% (Reels/Stories UI).
- A 1:1 crop of the centre must still work.
- No audio track. Music is added in Meta.

**Structure of every video**

| Time | Part | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0.0–3.0 s | HOOK | Strongest motion in the first 0.5 s. Each video gets two different hooks (Hook A and Hook B); the rest of the video is identical. |
| 3.0–8.0 s | BODY | Product story. |
| 8.0–end | END CARD | Logo, offer and CTA on a still or near-still frame. |

**People**
- NO FACES, ever. Only hands, forearms, torso from the chin down, or full backlit silhouettes.
- Hands: male, natural, well-groomed, no rings, no watch, no nail polish. Exactly 5 fingers per hand.
- Hands must interact physically correctly with fabric, ribbon, paper and box lids: weight, friction, folds.

**Product fidelity (zero tolerance)**
- The only garment is the **Milano Cashmere Lounge Set**: a zip-up knit jacket with a stand-up collar and silver zip, worn over a plain white crew-neck tee, and matching knit drawstring trousers with silver aglet tips. Cashmere-blend knit.
- Colours: **Beige, Gray, Black, Navy**. Match the reference photos exactly in colour, knit structure, zip, collar, cuffs, drawstrings and aglets.
- References:
  - Beige: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1009/1197/2689/files/beige_lounge.png
  - Gray: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1009/1197/2689/files/gray_lounge.png
  - Black: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1009/1197/2689/files/black_lounge.png
  - Navy: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1009/1197/2689/files/navy_lounge.png
- No logos, labels, tags, prints or embroidery on the garments.
- No garments, shoes or accessories from any other brand. No other clothing in frame at all, except the plain white tee.
- No morphing: the garment, its colour and the number of items must stay identical from the first to the last frame. Folded stacks keep the same order and the same count.
- **Wool socks** (the Black Week gift): plain cream ribbed wool socks, rolled and tied with natural twine. No logo, no pattern, no tag.

**Packaging and props**
- Matte black rigid gift box with a separate lid, black tissue paper, wide cream satin ribbon.
- Sealed black envelope with a plain cream wax seal (no letters or symbols).
- Props may only be: linen, oak, walnut, travertine, black marble, candles, fir sprigs, espresso cup.

**Look**
- Warm, quiet luxury: beige, cream, brown, black, gold.
- Real light: window light, firelight, a single spotlight.
- Shallow depth of field and natural film grain.
- Slow, deliberate camera: dolly, push-in, gentle orbit. Hooks may be faster.
- Physically plausible everything: no floating objects, no lids opening by themselves, no impossible light.
- Snow and rain must fall realistically.

**Text, logo and prices (critical)**
- NEVER generate text, numbers, prices or logos inside the footage. Render all text as clean overlay graphics in post, so they are pixel-sharp and spelled exactly as written here.
- Logo: use only the official file.
  - White version: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1009/1197/2689/files/ChatGPT_Image_Apr_16_2026_10_13_22_AM.png
  - Black version: https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/1009/1197/2689/files/ChatGPT_Image_Apr_15_2026_11_32_53_AM_879719be-a33e-4597-8534-a55edc225097.png
  - Never redraw, retype or stylise the logo.
  - Under the logo, in small tracked capitals (Montserrat Medium, letter-spacing about 40%): `TIMELESS. REFINED. YOU.`
- Typography:
  - Headlines and offers: Oswald Bold, all caps.
  - Supporting lines: Montserrat Medium.
  - Colour: white with a soft shadow on dark scenes; dark brown #26201A on light scenes.
  - Accent colour for dates and urgency: gold #D6B680.
- Use the prices and texts below exactly as written. No other claims. Never write "100% cashmere"; the product is a cashmere blend.

**Forbidden (reject the shot)**
- Faces
- Extra or melted fingers
- Garment colour shift, or a changing number of sets
- Garbled or AI-generated text
- Visible brand names or shop signs in backgrounds
- Generic stock-video look
- Cartoonish snow
- Plastic-looking skin
- Logos on garments
