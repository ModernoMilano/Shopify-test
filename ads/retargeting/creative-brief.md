# Creative brief: ModernoMilano retargeting

You are our creative strategist. Below are the facts from our Shopify and Meta data. Use them to design a set of retargeting creatives. Push back where you think we are wrong.

## The campaign
- **Goal:** convert people who already know us and almost bought. This is not about introducing the brand.
- **Audience:** Add to cart and Initiate checkout in the last 30 days, plus product viewers (90 days) and video viewers (120 days). Buyers from the last 180 days are excluded.
- **Countries:** US, UK, Ireland, Germany, Austria, Italy, Spain, Norway, Poland, Luxembourg. Germany and the US are big, so plan English and German versions.
- **Budget:** €40/day. That is a small pool (about 35k people), so creatives wear out fast.
- **Placements:** Facebook and Instagram feed, stories and reels. We need 9:16 and 4:5 for every concept.

## What the data tells us
- **The old retargeting failed on creative.** ROAS was 1.99, almost break-even (1.93). People saw our ads almost 4 times on average (frequency 3.8), and they were the same ads we use for cold traffic. Warm people saw nothing new.
- **Best sellers (Shopify):**

  | Product | Price | Orders |
  |---|---|---|
  | Cashmere Lounge Set Beige | €150 (was €200) | DE 74, US 50, UAE 7 |
  | Cashmere Lounge Set Black / Gray | €150 (was €200) | |
  | Knitted Two-Tone Cashmere Set Dark Mocha | €250 | |
  | Reverso Set | €175 (was €300) | #1 in the UAE |
  | Hooded Jacket Champagne | €80 | best-performing ad overall |

- **Our best ads are simple:**
  - A clean studio product shot (Bestseller Jacket).
  - A cashmere bundle image with one short line of copy.
  - A cashmere set video, which made 8 purchases on €137 in retargeting.
  - The winning copy is 1–3 short sentences in one paragraph. No checklists, no blank lines.
- **What did not work:**
  - Clever angle concepts (gift, "easy", quiet luxury) got clicks but no sales.
  - Sending people to a collection page got clicks but almost no add-to-carts. Link to the product page.
- **Returns are about 13% of gross sales.** Fit and expectations are a real friction point.
- **Average order:** €150–€210 in most markets, €260 in the UAE, €400 in Luxembourg. This is a considered purchase.

## The job: remove the reason they didn't buy
We think the main reasons people hesitate are, roughly in this order:
1. **Price and value.** €150–€250 for a set.
2. **Fit and size.** Will it fit me, how does it look on a real body.
3. **Quality and trust.** Is the cashmere really soft, who is this brand.
4. **Risk.** Returns, delivery, payment.

Build concepts that each kill one of those. Our German "Kein Risiko. Nur Stil." Reverso image is the direction for point 4: product flat lay, one strong headline, three trust points, price with strike-through.

## What we need from you: 6–8 concepts
For each concept, give us:
- Which hesitation it solves
- Product
- Format: static or video (6–15 seconds, hook in the first second)
- Visual description
- Primary text (max 3 sentences, one paragraph)
- Headline (max 40 characters)
- Landing page: the product page
- EN and DE versions

Ideas we expect to see, but improve on them:
- **On-body try-on:** sizing shown, model height and size on screen.
- **Macro and texture video of the cashmere:** "feel it" content.
- **Unboxing and packaging.**
- **Price breakdown:** set price versus pieces bought separately.
- **Real customer reviews or UGC.** Real ones only. Tell us what you need and we will collect them.
- **The "Kein Risiko" trust format** for each best seller.

## Rules
- **Only make claims we can back:**
  - 30-day returns
  - Tracked shipping
  - Klarna, Apple Pay and Google Pay
  - Discounts exactly as they are in the store. "Up to 50% off" cashmere is true; "everything 50% off" is not.
  - Do not promise delivery times.
- **Keep prices out of the image where possible.** We sell in EUR, USD and GBP. If a price is in the image, make one version per currency.
- **Design for a refresh every 2 weeks.** The audience is small. Give us a first batch plus an idea of what batch 2 looks like.
- **Success means** retargeting ROAS of 3 or higher, and a frequency of 3 per week or less.
