The 48-hour rule. Every decision is based on the last 48 hours. After any change, a campaign is left alone for 48 hours. Scaling, cutting, pausing ads and launching new creatives all happen in the same cycle.

01 · Roles

Who does what

Owner
  • Makes sure there are always enough creatives for a new batch every 48h.
  • Shoots real-life videos.
  • Approves anything above the €2,000 cap.
Media buyer
  • Runs the ladder and the creative test every 48h, exactly as written.
  • Asks for new creatives in time, before the test runs dry.
  • Reports after every cycle.

03 · Creatives

Creative testing: always have winners ready

The Creative Test campaign runs at €250 per day, so every ad gets enough spend for a real verdict. Every 48 hours one batch comes in and the winners move out.

1
JudgeScore every ad in the test campaign on its numbers from the last 48 hours.
2
CutSwitch the losers off.
3
PromoteMove the winners into the scaling campaigns using their Post ID, then switch them off in the test campaign.
4
LaunchAdd the new batch. The budget now goes to the fresh creatives.

04 · Routine

The 48-hour checklist

  1. Same moment, every cycle. Open Ads Manager and set the date range to the last 48 hours.
  2. Campaigns. Put every campaign on the ladder: scale up, hold or scale down.
  3. Cap check. Make sure the total daily budget stays at or under €2,000.
  4. Scaling ads. Inside the scaling campaigns, switch off ads that hit the loser rule.
  5. Creative test. Judge, cut and promote, then launch the new batch.
  6. Report. Send the owner the update below within one hour.
