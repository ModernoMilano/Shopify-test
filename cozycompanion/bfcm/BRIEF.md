## Honesty rules

These are hard rules and apply to every asset.
- The discount is real and on top of the current prices. Never raise compare-at prices.
- No fake countdown resets.
- No "only X left" unless it is true.
- No reviews, ratings or invented quotes.
- Always state the tiers correctly: 10% off $75+, 15% off $150+, 20% off $250+. It applies automatically at checkout.
- COZY15 does not stack with the BFCM tiers. Don't advertise stacking.

## Look

Luxury, not a shouty red SALE.
- **Sets:** deep charcoal or black velvet, warm candlelight and soft window light, cream linen accents.
- **Gifts:** wrapped in cream or charcoal paper with **rose-gold satin ribbon**.
- **Typography:** rose-gold foil (#b76e79 with a light gradient) and cream (#f5f1ed) on charcoal (#1a1a1a). Headlines in Cormorant 600; labels in Jost 500, letter-spaced.
- **Lockup:** "BLACK FRIDAY WEEK" eyebrow, then the Cormorant headline, then the tier line.
- **Headline:** *Black Friday, the cozy way.*
- **Tier line:** *Save 10% · 15% · 20% on orders $75 · $150 · $250+*
- **Production:** every photo is new Nano Banana (nano_banana_2_1, 2k, thinking high) with the real packshot as image reference. Every photo passes social/QA.md. Text is always added afterwards with PIL, never generated.
