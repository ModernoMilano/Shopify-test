# Social image QA: every image must pass ALL checks before it is posted

Open the 4:5 preview (and compare it side by side with the packshot reference). One failure = regenerate.

**Product**
- It is exactly our product: same shape, colour, material, proportions and details as the packshot.
- No invented parts, missing parts, warped edges, melted or doubled objects.
- Any CozyCompanion label is small, straight and spelled correctly (or there is none). No other brand names or logos.

**Animals and people**
- Correct anatomy: four legs, two eyes, natural paws and tails, no extra or merged limbs, realistic fur.
- Faces and hands of any person look natural (five fingers, no distortion). No real or recognisable people.
- The animal looks calm, safe and well cared for (nothing dangerous, no distress).

**Text**
- Text in the image only if intended; every word correctly spelled English, sharp and readable.
- No gibberish lettering, watermarks, signatures or UI artefacts.

**Social-media worthy**
- Sharp at phone size, good light, clean composition, subject in the centre so the 4:5 crop keeps it whole.
- Fits the brand: warm cream / oak / linen / charcoal / rose-gold palette, quiet luxury, nothing cheap or cluttered.
- Would stop the scroll: a clear focal point and an emotion (cozy, funny, joyful, calm).

**Copy (caption)**
- English, warm and concise: hook line, 1–3 short lines, call to action ("Shop via the link in bio" /
  "cozycompanion.us"), 8–12 relevant hashtags. Max 2–3 emoji.
- No prices, no discount claims other than "15% off your first order with code COZY15", no health or safety
  claims beyond the product facts, no reviews, ratings or invented customer quotes.

Up to 3 attempts per image. If an image still fails after 3 attempts, use a different scene idea or product;
never post a failed image.
