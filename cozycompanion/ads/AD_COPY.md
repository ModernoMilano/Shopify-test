## Campaign setup (paused until the store is live)
- **Objective:** Sales (Purchase), pixel/CAPI events from Shopify.
- **Structure:** 1 campaign (Advantage+ campaign budget, $50/day) → 3 ad sets (one per product) → 5 ads each.
- **Audience:** United States, 25–65+, Advantage+ audience (broad); optional interest hint "Dog owners/Cat owners/Pets".
- **Placements:** Advantage+ placements (Feed, Reels, Stories).
- **After 4–7 days:** pause ads with CTR < 0.8% or no add-to-cart after ~$30 spend; move budget to the winners.
