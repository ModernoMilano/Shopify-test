You will look twice.

Nothing on him says a word.

And still, you look.

He never asked you to.

So where does that come from.

From the elegance of Milan... to the precision of the atelier.

A line in chalk.

Straight at the shoulder.

Cut once.

Room to breathe.

Thread... until it holds.

Pressed flat... until the seam forgets it was two.

Worn, until it forgets it was new.

One piece. ... Two sides.

The same man.

Two words.

Not an address.

Moderno is the man.

Milano is the measure.

Worn together.

The name comes last.

Inside. ... Against the neck.

You wear it. ... Nobody reads it.

Moderno Milano.
