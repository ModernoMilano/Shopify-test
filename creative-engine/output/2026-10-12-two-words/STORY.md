# Merkfilm "Two Words" (plan, 12 oktober 2026)

## Wat de eigenaar vroeg

- De tekst van "Let the Day Do the Dressing" vond hij "heel slecht". Hij wil echte brand storytelling, niet "my father".
- Meer nieuwe beelden: stoffen in een atelier, stof die op maat gesneden wordt, het label dat in een kledingstuk wordt genaaid. Het bestaande materiaal dient als opvulling.
- Een voorbeeld van hem: "From the elegance of Milan" (de Galleria) en "to the precision of the atelier" (precisiewerk).
- Alle beelden moeten passen bij de tekst die in beeld komt.
- Hij koopt 1000 credits. Het moet "ijzersterk" en "next level" worden, een video die viraal kan gaan door de perfectie.

## Hoe het script tot stand kwam (workflow, 8 agents op Fable)

- Research naar het maakverhaal bij Loro Piana, Zegna, Hermès, Cucinelli, Bottega Veneta, Berluti, Kiton, Asket en anderen, en naar wat craft-reels viraal maakt.
- Drie schrijvers schreven elk een versie: "From ... to ...", "Moderno Milano als idee" en "de naam binnenin".
- Drie beoordelaars gaven punten: copy chief, merk en claims, en editor met AI-video. Copy chief en editor kozen "Two Words" (48 en 50); de merkstrateeg zette hem één punt lager, op de tweede plaats.
- Een finisher voerde de verbeteringen door.

**Idee:** The brand name treated as an idea, not an address. The film opens on the owner's own pair ("From the elegance of Milan" over the Galleria dome already pushing in at frame 1; "To the precision of the atelier" over a stack of cloth squared flush by his hand) and runs one visual motif through everything: the line. Milan draws its lines in stone (the Galleria's inlaid floor, the one-point arcade he walks); the table draws them in chalk, follows them with shears, fixes them in thread and presses them flat; then a name is sewn inside the collar and the line leaves the table to be worn, one way and the other. The turn lands at 60% on the finished label: "Two words. Not an address." The last chapter answers the question the label opened: Moderno is the man (the lake), Milano is the measure (the dome at dusk), worn together (the man in the armchair with the city behind him). Every spoken line is literally the picture under it; every table shot starts from a finished state and moves only to reveal it (rule lifts off the chalk line, the cut piece lifts off the finished cut, the needle slows to a stop, the thumb smooths the label), so each cut lands as a small, oddly satisfying completion and nothing AI struggles with is asked to move. One garment goes through the whole table, the Knitted Two-Tone Cashmere set in Dark Mocha: the cream band stitched on at 26 s is the band on the hood edge at 41 s and the knit on his sleeve in the last frame. The day runs dawn under the dome to blue hour at the window, and the last frame (blue glass grid, Milan room tone) hands back to the first (blue glass dome, same tone) so the Reel loops as the day does. The voice never says who makes anything or where: "the atelier" is the owner's phrase, once, with the definite article; the one production verb is the passive, true "a name is sewn in".

## Nieuwe shots (12)

### N01 DOME DAWN (4 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Galleria Vittorio Emanuele II, interior, lens straight up from the floor at the exact centre of the octagon: the glass-and-iron dome dead centre and perfectly symmetrical, iron ribs radiating to the four corners of the 9:16 frame, the panes a deep dawn blue with the first cold light on the ironwork; the lunettes soft and unreadable; no shop level, no signage, no people. Wide lens (about 24 mm equivalent), centred composition. Grade cool blue-hour with ivory highlights and match frame 1 exactly to the last frame of HOME B (same blue, same exposure) so the loop is window-to-dome.
- **Beweging:** One slow, dead-straight push toward the dome's centre, about 6 percent scale over 4 s, no roll, no tilt; the sky warms by one step from blue to grey-blue. Frame 1 is already moving.
- **Geluid:** Dawn arcade room tone with long reverb; the Milan evening bed from HOME B's last frame carries across the loop seam and thins into morning; one pigeon's wings high in the dome; one distant footstep.
- **Risico:** Medium: iron ribs bending or multiplying, invented lettering in the lunettes. Push only (never rotate: rotation bends the ribs), tiny move, frame on the glass centre; step every frame and reject any take where a rib curves or a pane flickers. Zero-risk fallback: the 4K still with the push done in the edit. This is the hook and the loop frame: second call on the redo reserve after N10. Verify frame 1 against HOME B's last frame side by side before the grade is locked.

### N02 FLOOR GLIDE (4 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Camera 30 cm above the Galleria's polished marble inlay floor, looking along the main arcade toward the octagon at dawn; the geometric inlay (cream, grey, black and terracotta marble) runs in straight bands to the vanishing point with one centre line running straight up the frame; the lamps reflect in the polish; the arcade empty except two blurred figures far away with their backs to camera; shopfronts far, dark and out of focus, no readable signs; not the bull mosaic, no coats of arms. About 35 mm, horizon locked.
- **Beweging:** Camera only: a slow glide forward along the centre line at a slow walking pace; the inlay lines converge; nothing else moves.
- **Geluid:** Arcade hush, a soft stone echo, two distant unhurried footsteps; the reverb thins toward the cut.
- **Risico:** Low: the inlay may shimmer or morph, the far figures may drift. Camera-only motion, low and slow; paint the figures out in post if they misbehave. Fallback: a slow push on the 4K still. Chosen over a loafer-landing macro because the converging lines illustrate 'draws its lines in stone' literally and cannot fail.

### N03 GALLERIA WALK (3.5 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Wide, symmetrical one-point perspective down the main arcade toward the octagon in first light; he is seen from behind at mid-distance, dead centre, walking away on the mosaic floor in the Milano Reverso gilet in anthracite worn anthracite side out over an anthracite knit, dark trousers, grey suede loafers (the same look as the RAIN footage); shafts of morning light across the floor; shopfronts dark and soft, no legible signs; at most two distant blurred figures far ahead, backs to camera; no accessories, no face. About 35 mm, camera static at chest height. Generated at 4 s, used 3.5.
- **Beweging:** He takes three or four slow, even steps away, posture upright, pace unhurried; camera static.
- **Geluid:** Even loafer steps receding on marble with the arcade's echo, morning air; the reverb thins toward the hard cut into the dry atelier.
- **Risico:** Medium: gait stutter, feet sliding, hallucinated lettering on the shop fronts. Back view hides the face, static camera limits drift, the 480p draft decides; cull any take with a readable sign or a limb stutter. Fallback at zero cost: RAIN trimmed to 3.5 s (stone arcade, the look-up) carries the same line.

### N04 THE STACK (3.5 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Atelier cutting table, soft north window light from the left: a stack of folded cashmere cloth in the brand's colours only (crema, pietra, cammello, dark mocha, bordeaux, antracite, nero), dark mocha on top, edges nearly flush; his right hand already rests on the top fold at frame 1, cuff of an anthracite knit sleeve; plain shears and a wooden rule out of focus at the frame edge; no tags, tickets, printed selvedge, paper or logos. About 50 mm macro, slightly high angle, off-centre composition.
- **Beweging:** His hand pushes the top fold about 2 cm until its edge sits exactly flush with the stack, then lifts away. Ends perfectly squared, nothing moving.
- **Geluid:** Room tone drops to a close dry interior on the cut; a cloth compress hush as the fold is pushed flush; a soft wooden table creak.
- **Risico:** Low to medium: hand morphing, colour drift between folds. Hand on the cloth at frame 1 so nothing enters the frame; one push, one lift. Reject any take where the edges do not finish aligned; the alignment is the whole shot. Also the natural stand-in frame if any later atelier macro fails.

### N05 TAPE (3.5 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Macro, top-down, soft daylight: a length of dark mocha knit cloth lies flat on the table, the fine dense knit visible in raking light; a plain cream tailor's tape laid straight across it, printed side down and outside the focus plane so no digit is readable; his fingertips rest on the tape's near end at frame 1, anthracite cuff.
- **Beweging:** His fingertips smooth the tape flat along the cloth in one slow pass of about 8 cm and lift away; the tape lies dead straight.
- **Geluid:** Tape sliding over knit, one fingertip press; near silence under the written pause.
- **Risico:** Low to medium: tape numerals count as text under the brand rule (printed side down or a plain tape; check every frame for digits); fingers can grow or merge (one slow pass only). Fallback: slow push-in on the still with the fingers resting on the tape.

### N06 THE LINE (3.5 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Macro on the dark mocha knit cloth: a plain bevelled steel rule with no scale lying along a straight white chalk line already drawn, a flat triangle of tailor's chalk resting beside it; soft side light so the chalk reads as a crisp white edge; his hand holds the rule at its near end, anthracite cuff.
- **Beweging:** His hand lifts the rule straight up and out of frame, revealing the one clean chalk line; the chalk does not move. (Second seed only: the chalk completes the last 3 cm along the rule.)
- **Geluid:** A dry chalk scrape of about 0.4 s just before the rule lifts (the drawing lives in the sound); the rule set down off-frame with a soft steel tick.
- **Risico:** Medium if the chalk draws, low as a reveal: the line can wobble, thicken or vanish when the rule lifts. The start image holds the finished line, the rule lifts vertically only; reject any take where the line changes. Rule markings would be text: plain edge, no scale.

### N07 THE CUT (3.5 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Macro at 45 degrees: plain black-handled tailor's shears (no engraving) resting closed at the end of the chalk line on the dark mocha knit cloth, the cut already made and lying closed along the line; his hand holds the corner of the cut piece at frame 1; the steel rule set aside at the frame edge; same window light.
- **Beweging:** His hand lifts the cut piece slowly about 5 cm, peeling it from the cloth to reveal the straight cut edge sitting exactly on the chalk line; the shears never move. Ends with the clean edge in full view.
- **Geluid:** One clean shear stroke through cloth at the cut-in (the cut that has just happened), then the whisper of the piece lifting; nothing else.
- **Risico:** AI video fails at blades cutting cloth, so the cut is finished before frame 1 and only the lift moves. Knit edges can curl: specify a dense flat knit and a small piece. Shears carry no mark. A moving-blade version (blades already in the cloth, one short close) is generated only with credits left after all twelve shots are approved and used only if every frame is flawless.

### N08 THE STITCH (3.5 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Extreme macro: a sewing-machine needle and presser foot where a cream knit band meets the dark mocha knit edge (the band of the Two-Tone set), cream thread forming a straight seam that runs toward camera, about 10 cm of seam already behind the foot; shallow depth of field, warm lamp light; only the foot, needle and plate visible, no badge, no dials, no lettering.
- **Beweging:** The needle rises and falls at a slow, even tempo (four to six stitches in the shot) while the feed moves the cloth a few millimetres each time; on the last frame the needle stops in the up position.
- **Geluid:** The machine at walking pace, clean, no rattle; the stitches placed on the words 'One' and 'next'; a breath of room tone in the pause; stops with the picture.
- **Risico:** Medium: needle doubling, thread continuity, cloth sliding sideways. Slow speed, one needle, shallow focus, dark cloth with light thread; reject any take where the stitch line drifts off straight. The machine implies making; the line over it speaks only of the stitch.

### N09 THE PRESS (4 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Macro from a low angle: a plain matte steam iron (no logo, no text, no dials in frame) resting on the cream-on-mocha seam from N08 on a pressing board, the seam slightly raised ahead of the sole and flat behind it; his hand on the handle, anthracite cuff; a first small breath of steam; warm side light so the steam reads.
- **Beweging:** The iron glides about 5 cm along the seam with one soft puff of steam; the cloth goes flat in its wake; the iron lifts slightly at the end and holds. Ends with a straight, flat, centred seam, the match-cut source for HOME C.
- **Geluid:** One long steam hiss under the line, the weight of the iron settling, cloth under it; the hiss thins to silence as the seam settles.
- **Risico:** Low to medium: steam rendering as smoke, the iron warping or sprouting lettering. One small puff, slow glide, plain matte iron; check for text every frame. The last frame must be straight and centred at the same scale as HOME C's cream band or the match cut fails.

### N10 LABEL SEWN (3.5 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Extreme macro at the machine: the ivory satin ModernoMilano neck label (black script composited from brand/assets/necklabel.jpg, upright, centred, sharp at 4K) positioned inside the neck of the dark mocha hooded knit; the needle and presser foot at the label's top edge, well away from the letters, three stitches already in; his fingertips steadying the label at its lower corners; the machine unbranded and mostly out of frame; warm side light on the satin. No size or care label in frame.
- **Beweging:** Three or four slow stitches along the label's top edge; the label advances under 5 mm; the script itself stays still and sharp; the presser foot lifts a few millimetres at the end and the needle stops.
- **Geluid:** The machine very slow, the last stitches, then the presser-foot click.
- **Risico:** Highest in the film: legible text is what AI video breaks first (earlier batches produced misspelled and upside-down labels). Build the start image from the real label photo; keep the script centre-frame and still with motion only at the edge; three seeds minimum; check spelling and orientation on first, middle and last frame at 4K; never place a subtitle card over the label. First call on the redo reserve. Fallback: needle down and still, the fingertips smooth the label once.

### N11 LABEL FLAT (4 s)
- **Startbeeld:** The finished neck of the dark mocha knit laid open on the table, slightly wider than N10: the ivory satin label sewn in and perfectly flat, satin catching the light, the black script crisp and fully legible, thread ends trimmed, the cream band of the hood edge just visible beside it; his thumb resting at the label's left edge at frame 1.
- **Beweging:** A very slow push-in of about 4 percent while his thumb smooths the satin once from left to right and rests; the sheen shifts across the script; then nothing moves.
- **Geluid:** One thread snip just before the line; satin under the thumb; then near silence; the written pauses are empty.
- **Risico:** Medium: script wobble or satin sheen swallowing letters under motion. Minimal motion, constant light. The accepted fallback is equal in quality: the 4K still with the push done in the edit. This is the turn of the film and the brand's only mark; nothing less than perfect is accepted.

### N12 DOME DUSK (3.5 s)
- **Startbeeld:** Same framing as N01 (floor level, lens straight up, dome dead centre, ribs to the corners) at dusk: deep blue sky through the panes, the arcade lamps lit so the ironwork glows warm against the blue; no people, no signage, lunettes soft. Graded to sit between LAKE A's gold and HOME B's blue.
- **Beweging:** Slow pull-back, the mirror of N01's push; nothing else moves.
- **Geluid:** Evening arcade room tone, one far church bell, two unhurried footsteps.
- **Risico:** Medium, as N01. Generated last of the twelve. If the reserve is spent: fallback 1 is N01 reversed (a pull-back) regraded warm with the lamp glow lifted; fallback 2 at zero cost is RAIN (Milan stone arcade, the measured interval of its columns, him in the anthracite gilet). The line 'Milano is the measure' never plays over an unrelated frame. Budget: 12 shots at about 66 credits is about 792, leaving about 208 for three redos in the order N10, N01, N11; N11 as a still and N12 as RAIN each return a shot's worth of credits to the reserve.

## Claims

De stem zegt nergens wie iets maakt of waar. Hij zegt nooit "our", "made", "handmade", "artisans", "Italy" en noemt geen jaartal. "The atelier" komt één keer voor, als de woorden van de eigenaar. De zin over het label is nu "The name comes last." (eigenaar, 12 okt; was "Last, a name is sewn in."). De winkeldata zegt niets over herkomst of productie.

Milano is the measure.
