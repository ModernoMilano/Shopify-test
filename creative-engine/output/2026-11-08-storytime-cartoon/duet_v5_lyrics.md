# Duet tekst v5 (gepolijste versie uit de schrijverskamer, nog niet gegenereerd): "Make an Effort"

Alle 22 rapregels 10 lettergrepen, rijmschema per couplet AABB / ABAB / AABB met zuivere rijmen (collar/scholar, throat/quote,
screen/scene; sun/one, date/straight, together/weather, sound/found; sister/mister, slate/eight, meeting/eating, high/guy),
binnenrijm (door/floor, pay/day, man/plan, made/paid), merknaam 5x (3x gezongen), haar stukken ongewijzigd. Checker: geslaagd.

Stijlprompt: Modern R&B, 88 BPM half-time, strong snare on 2 and 4, warm Rhodes chords, soft 808 bass, finger snaps, nylon-string guitar and soft strings with Italian warmth. Two voices. The male rapper is relaxed, dry and self-deprecating with clear, even diction; the rapper locks to the grid, one line per bar, never rushed, punchlines landing on the last beat. The female singer has a beautiful warm, honest, soulful voice, intimate with light vibrato; sections tagged female vocal are sung entirely by her; duet lines are sung together. No intro, vocals on the first beat; no outro. Pronounce Moderno Milano the Italian way: mo-DER-no mi-LA-no.
