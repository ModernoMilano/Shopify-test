=== PART A ===
[Intro]
Three years. Same table. Her birthday.
She looks at me and goes...

[Verse 1]
You used to make an effort. That's it, that's the line,
Said it over the cake while her mum poured the wine.
Same grey hoodie I'd been wearing since Tuesday,
Hole in the knee like it had something to say.
Her friends in dresses, I'm dressed for the couch,
She smiled at the waiter, not at me. Ouch.
Two weeks later she's gone, no shouting, no fight,
Packed a bag while I'm gaming, left a note by the light.

[Verse 2]
Then I saw them. Corso Como. Little cafe in the sun,
Him with an espresso like he'd already won.
He wasn't richer, I checked. Same job as me.
Wasn't taller, wasn't funnier, honestly.
Navy gilet, knit underneath, trousers that fit,
Loafers, no socks, and that was it.
He looked like a man who had his life together,
I looked like a man who got dressed for the weather.
She laughed at something he said, and I swear I forgot
That she laughed like that. I forgot what we'd got.

=== PART B ===
[Verse 3]
So I tried. Bought a suit, wore it to work on a Monday,
Boss goes interview? I was back in the hoodie by one day.
Tried the influencer look: chains, tight tee, sneakers like bread,
My sister goes you selling crypto? and went back to bed.
Day one of giving up, hoodie on, head down, same street,
And there he is again. Same gilet. Same calm. Same feet.
I did the most embarrassing thing I've done in my life:
I asked the man who took my girl, bro, where's the fit from?

[Verse 4]
He didn't laugh. He said, mate, it's not the gilet,
It's that everything fits and nothing's got anything to say.
Two words: Moderno Milano. Designed in Milan, nothing on the chest,
Cashmere blends, two sets in the wardrobe, forget the rest.
So I ordered one. Navy. Hundred and fifty, done.
Less than the chains that made my sister run.

=== PART C ===
[Verse 5]
Day one: took the bins out, flatmate stops mid-chew,
Who are you meeting? Nobody, Dave. The bins. You?
Day four: barista knows my name, four years I've been there,
Day nine: colleague asks if I got promoted. Same desk. Same chair.
Day twelve: the joggers with the hole went in the bin,
Didn't even feel it. Didn't even flinch.
Day sixteen: she texts. Three words. You made an effort.
Read it twice. Put the phone down. Had plans. I kept it.

[Verse 6]
Here's what I learned, and it's cheaper than therapy:
Nobody's judging your face or your salary,
They're reading the effort. Moderno Milano, no logo, no noise,
A full set's a hundred and fifty, and it's Black Week, boys:
Two sets fifteen off, three sets twenty, wool socks in the box,
I rotate navy, grey and beige. Even my sister stopped.

[Outro]
If you ever heard you used to make an effort and it stung,
That's your sign. Link's below. Moderno Milano. Go make one.
Make an effort. It's cheaper than being left.
Moderno Milano. Timeless. Refined. You.
